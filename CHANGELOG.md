# Changelog

## 0.9.0 beta (first public release)

- Named legends rise among the Scrapyard raiders. Each has a home camp, strengths and weaknesses, a title card and taunts, and a journal (L) for what you know about them.
- Fame from their own deeds off-screen. The fame levels are Rumor, Known, Feared and Infamous, and each level brings more health, extra strengths and better weapons.
- News and rumors at Coyote traders, in Wild Wasteland 2's talk window or with T.
- Memory and grudges: legends taunt you about earlier fights, and a big grudge sends one hunting you.
- Comebacks: a killed legend can return with a scar and a new title, but never after a third death.
- Road ambushes on Wild Wasteland 2 raider patrols, and map and compass markers for known camps.
- Rewards: the mark's bounty (100–600 nuts), a trophy weapon with a damage bonus and perks (Bleeding, Quick, Skullsplitter, Bloodthirst), and the camp's stash, which always has armor plates.
- Car theft, off by default.
- Every setting is in Apocasetter (F6, then Mods), including an Enabled switch that puts legends away without losing them.
- Names over marks and trophies on the ground only show for the one you look at (ItemLabels: LookAt, Always or Off).
